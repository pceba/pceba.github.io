I.

Where I think this is going

Models are moving from answering questions to carrying out work: planning a task, using tools, checking the result. I spent this summer building one of these systems for credit risk. A few things I took away from it.

  1. 01

    From answers to actions

    The shift isn't a smarter chatbot. It's software that can take a goal, break it into steps and execute them. The hard question stops being can the model do it and becomes can we trust it to.

  2. 02

    The model is the easy part

    In a bank, most of the effort goes into data quality, governance and validation. An agent is only as useful as the data it reads and the controls around what it does.

  3. 03

    Experts stay in the loop

    The most useful agents I can picture make a specialist faster rather than replacing them: they do the repetitive groundwork and leave the judgement calls to a person.
