About me

Data, machine learning and AI are going to change what a normal working day looks like in most companies.

I study Computer Science and Business Administration at the same time, and the part I enjoy most is the space between them: understanding what a business actually needs, and knowing enough about the technology to build it.

In Data & Analytics at Oliver Wyman I saw a small version of what is coming. Working with AI coding agents, I built a tool that gathers the repetitive groundwork of creating a credit-risk model in one place, so the team can spend its time on judgement. I think a lot of knowledge work will move in that direction: less time gathering and cleaning information, more time deciding what to do with it.

The organisations that get the most out of this will be the ones with people who understand both sides. That is the kind of role I am working towards.
